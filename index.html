KNOW YOUR BLOOD GROUP

Blood group compatibility guide

A quick educational reference for red blood cell (RBC) compatibility.

O−

Receive: O−

Donate to: All 8 groups

O+

Receive: O−, O+

Donate to: O+, A+, B+, AB+

A−

Receive: O−, A−

Donate to: A−, A+, AB−, AB+

A+

Receive: O−, O+, A−, A+

Donate to: A+, AB+

B−

Receive: O−, B−

Donate to: B−, B+, AB−, AB+

B+

Receive: O−, O+, B−, B+

Donate to: B+, AB+

AB−

Receive: O−, A−, B−, AB−

Donate to: AB−, AB+

AB+

Receive: All 8 groups

Donate to: AB+ only

Educational information only. A qualified clinician must assess every transfusion, including blood typing and crossmatching. This guide covers red blood cell compatibility only, not plasma or whole blood.
